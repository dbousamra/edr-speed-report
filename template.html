Team lap explorer

Head-to-head comparison of every driver's best clean lap in each car + track combo. Filter, slice and exclude drivers below; everything recalculates.

How the numbers work
  • Each driver's best clean lap per car + track combo (Garage61 "clean" flag).
  • Only combos with at least Min drivers / combo drivers count.
  • Within a combo, every driver is compared to every other driver. Faster lap wins that matchup.
  • H2H win %: share of matchups won. 50% = average. A combo with N drivers gives each driver N−1 matchups.
  • Typical gap: median % behind the fastest lap in each combo (0.5% ≈ 0.5s on a 100s lap).
  • Avg finish: average position within each combo, 100% = fastest, 0% = slowest; every combo weighted equally.
  • Fastest laps / Drivers beaten: combos where the driver holds the fastest lap, and how many drivers those laps beat.
  • Excluding a driver removes them from every combo, so others' results recalculate without them.
  • Best laps reflect seat time as well as pace; conditions, setups and track changes aren't controlled for.
