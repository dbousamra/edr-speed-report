Who's actually fastest?

Records alone lie. This ranks drivers by how many teammates they beat, head-to-head, in the same car on the same track.

How it works

  • Every driver's best clean lap in every car + track combo from the team's Garage61 data.
  • Combos with fewer than 3 drivers are thrown out. No padding stats in a car nobody else drives.
  • Inside each combo, everyone goes head-to-head with everyone else. Faster lap wins.
  • H2H win % = share of head-to-heads you win. 50% is team average. Beating 10 people in a busy combo counts 9×; beating 2 counts 2×.
  • You need 5+ qualifying combos to be ranked.
  • Typical gap = your median % behind the fastest lap in each combo (0.5% ≈ 0.5s on a 100s lap).

Caveats

  • A best lap reflects seat time as much as talent. Someone who did 200 laps will beat someone who did 5.
  • Laps come from every condition and season. Track evolution, setups and weather aren't controlled for.
  • Laps Garage61 flags as not clean are excluded.
  • Small samples swing. Check the Matchups column before trash-talking.
